# Branding and domain decisions

## Locked product identity

**Oryveta** (previous working name: MASGENT). Brand statement: **Build thoughtfully. Evolve continuously.** Short UI tagline: **Build. Experiment. Prove.**

Two primary entry points: **Start New** and **Evolve**. Minimalist **Soft Modern** design. Avoid names that imply Kaggle-only functionality.

## Domain

Production-style free Vercel preview alias: https://oryveta.vercel.app — assigned and verified by the Vercel integration on 2026-10-09. Public browser fetch was not independently verified from this assistant session.

Earlier Namecheap checks reported oryveta.com available around $10.98 for the initial year and oryveta.xyz around $2 first year; these are **historical quotes**, availability/prices can change and domains renew rather than transfer ownership forever. No Oryveta paid domain purchase has been made.

## Brand expression

Warm off-white, calm surfaces, restrained blue accent, purposeful smooth motion, minimal visual noise; avoid excessive glass effects and inflated AI claims.
